The Oak Residence

Location Banjara Hills, Hyderabad
Typology Private Residential Estate
Scale & Scope 680 m² (Turnkey Interior)
Completion Autumn 2025
Design Intent

Geological Permanence & Continuous Timber

Situated on a quiet rise in Banjara Hills, The Oak Residence was conceived as an architectural counterweight to the pace of the city. The brief called for a sanctuary that felt rooted in the Deccan plateau, using monolithic proportions and a restrained palette of Indian teak, local limestone, and hand-patinated brass.

Rather than relying on walls to divide the 680-square-meter floor plan, HUASAN introduced continuous architectural millwork that folds seamlessly from ceiling claddings into full-height storage, concealed doors, and integrated lighting channels.

"The transitions between room volumes were calibrated so that daylight leads the inhabitant forward like water carving through stone."

— Julian Vance, Design Principal
