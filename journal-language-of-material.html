Materials

The Language of Material

Words by Kabir Menon • Photography by Arjun Rao

Location

Banjara Hills, Hyderabad

Project Type

Material Essay

Materials

Teak, limestone, linen, brass

Materials speak before furniture does. A honed limestone stair in Hyderabad already tells you how the house wants to be touched, how it wants to age, and how quietly it intends to live.

We prefer surfaces that keep a memory: open-pore teak, unfilled stone, brass that darkens at the handle. These are not rustic gestures. They are a refusal of the plastic permanence that dates a room in a single season.

"Indian craft already knows this: a slightly irregular glaze is more honest than a perfect one."

In our studios, samples are laid like sentences. Teak against lime wash. Brass against ivory plaster. The wrong pairing is immediately loud. The right one almost disappears, which is the point.

"Permanence is not a colour. It is a material that is allowed to keep living."

Specify fewer things, specify them well, and let the Deccan climate finish the work. Heat, dust, and monsoon will do what catalogues cannot.
